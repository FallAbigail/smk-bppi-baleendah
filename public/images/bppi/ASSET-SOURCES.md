# SMK BPPI image assets

- `teachers/teacher-01.jpg` through `teacher-34.jpg` and `teachers/leader-01.jpg` through `leader-03.jpg` come from the public staff photos on https://smk.sekolahbppi.sch.id/tim-sekolah. Portraits were resized locally for page performance; their order follows the official page.
- `programs/akl.png`, `programs/pplg.png`, `programs/tjkt.png`, and `programs/acp.png` are the corresponding official program logos from https://smk.sekolahbppi.sch.id/jurusan/1 through `/jurusan/4`.
- `hero-pra-mpls-poster.png` is the poster image supplied by the site owner and used as the visual reference for the hero characters.
- `hero-students-transparent.png` is the transparent student cutout used in the hero; the poster background, copy, and partner marks are removed.
- `../../videos/smk-bppi-hero.mp4` is the official SMK BPPI homepage hero video, copied from https://smk.sekolahbppi.sch.id/vid/vidhome.mp4 for local playback.
